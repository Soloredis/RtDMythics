# Info

RtDMythics is a magic-focused overhaul built around hunting, lore, and a slow burn into real magical power. Expect pixie queens, restless spirits, and a story that rewards anyone willing to dig for it.

* All magical weapons have special secondary attacks
* Start magic after defeating Eikthyr.
* Magical healing staves that will help guide you on your journey
* Magical food that progresses with you throughout the world
* Multiple unique magic armor sets
* Adds custom set effects to many Vanilla sets that don't already have one
* 8 Pixie minibosses with unique progression drops, spawning every 30 minutes at glowing runestones
* Hunt the undead zombies at night for essence, but beware, they carry possessing spirits both good or bad
* Undead runestones with a somber story, spawning every 30 minutes regardless of time of day
* Unique lore with a hidden story, can you find all the lore?
* 8 custom totems to summon friendly fae to heal and protect you
* Multiple unique zombies, when they die a soul is released
* Combine all RtD mods to have a fully developed Deep North!

# Hints

* To start magical progression you must first slay Eikthyr to obtain his trophy
* There are 36 Pixie Queen runestones per biome, slay the queen for magical crystals to advance your progression
* To obtain magical spirit essence you must venture out at night to hunt the dead, the dead have a 100% chance to drop 1 essence
* All undead have a 40% chance to release a tormented or friendly spirit
* Each spirit has a 100% chance for 2 essence
* Consume magical essence in the first biome to obtain Eitr, once in BlackForest craft a cauldron and plant some carrots!
